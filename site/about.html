Our Hospital

About Bhute Hospital

A specialist hospital in Akola, Maharashtra, built around one idea: clinical decisions made by qualified specialists, explained clearly, and tailored to the individual patient.

Who We Are

Bhute Hospital provides endoscopic spine, orthopaedic, trauma, and dental care to the people of Akola and the surrounding region. Our primary clinical focus is advanced spine care, led by Dr. Abhinav Bhute, who trained in orthopaedics and completed fellowship training in spine surgery. Dental care is led by Dr. Trupti Bhute.

We believe good medicine starts with listening. Assessment comes before answers; options come before decisions; and no plan is final until the patient understands and agrees to it.
