Speciality

Orthopaedic Care in Akola

Assessment and treatment of bones, joints, and musculoskeletal conditions, coordinated with our spine and trauma services and supported by planned rehabilitation.

What We Assess

Orthopaedic consultations cover the full range of musculoskeletal complaints. After examination, appropriate imaging is arranged as indicated, and options are explained clearly before treatment begins.

Not every listed service is available or appropriate in every case; treatment options vary between patients and depend on clinical assessment.

Areas of Care

Conditions We See

Joint pain

Assessment of painful joints and their underlying causes.

Fractures

Diagnosis and management of broken bones, coordinated with our trauma pathway.

Sports injuries

Assessment of injuries from sport or activity, with planned recovery.

Arthritis

Care for degenerative joint conditions focused on function and comfort.

Shoulder conditions

Assessment of shoulder pain, stiffness, and injuries.

Knee conditions

From injuries to degenerative change, examined and explained.

Musculoskeletal pain

General muscle, tendon, and bone complaints.

Rehabilitation planning

Recovery planning with appropriate physiotherapy input.
