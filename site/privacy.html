Privacy Policy

How information you share through this website is handled.

What We Collect

If you request an appointment through this website, you may share your name, mobile number, preferred speciality and date, and an optional message. This is used only to contact you and arrange your appointment.

What We Do Not Collect

This website is static and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. We do not collect medical information through the website, and we ask you not to send sensitive medical details through the appointment form.

Retention and Sharing

Appointment request information is retained only as long as needed to arrange your appointment and is not shared with third parties except where required by law.

Your Choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the details you shared in an appointment request by contacting the hospital.

Updates

This policy may be updated as hospital contact and appointment systems are finalised. Please contact us with any questions about privacy.
