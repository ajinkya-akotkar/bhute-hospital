Our Primary Clinical Focus

Endoscopic Spine Care in Akola

Spine care at Bhute Hospital is led by Dr. Abhinav Bhute, whose clinical interests include endoscopic and minimally invasive spine techniques, Unilateral Biportal Endoscopy (UBE), navigation-supported procedures, and the assessment of complex and revision spine conditions.

Consultation & Diagnosis

Every spine consultation begins with listening: your history, symptoms, goals, and concerns. Clinical examination and appropriate imaging are arranged as indicated, and findings are explained in plain language.

Many spinal conditions are managed without surgery. Where surgery may be appropriate, the reason, alternatives, benefits, and risks are discussed before any decision is made. Suitability for any technique — endoscopic or otherwise — always depends on clinical assessment.

Treatment Approaches

Options Considered, One Patient at a Time

Activity guidance, physiotherapy input, and medication where appropriate are considered first for many spinal conditions. Surgery is one option among several, not a default.

Endoscopic techniques use small incisions and a camera to address selected spinal conditions. They may be appropriate for selected patients, depending on clinical assessment and imaging.

UBE is an endoscopic approach that works through two small portals, allowing visualisation and work with fine instruments. Suitability depends on the diagnosis and clinical assessment.

Minimally invasive approaches aim to reduce disruption to healthy tissue for selected procedures. Whether they are appropriate varies between patients and conditions.

Intraoperative imaging and navigation can support precision for selected spine procedures, where clinically appropriate.

Previously operated or complicated spine conditions require particularly careful assessment. Options are discussed honestly, including what surgery can and cannot achieve.

When to Seek Care Promptly

Spine Warning Signs

The following symptoms can indicate a condition that needs prompt or urgent medical evaluation. If you experience any of these, seek medical assessment without delay:

  • New or worsening weakness in the legs or difficulty walking
  • Numbness in the genital or saddle area
  • New loss of bladder or bowel control
  • Severe, unrelenting night pain or pain with fever and feeling unwell
  • Back pain after a significant fall or accident

This list is not exhaustive. If you are unsure, contact a doctor. This page is general education and does not diagnose conditions; a consultation is required for individual advice.
