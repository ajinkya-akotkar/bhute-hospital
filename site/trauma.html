Speciality

Orthopaedic Trauma Care in Akola

Fracture and musculoskeletal injury care with careful assessment, clear treatment decisions, and planned follow-up — coordinated with our orthopaedic and spine services.

Important

In an Emergency

If you or someone nearby has a suspected serious injury — a visible deformity, bone through skin, inability to bear weight after a fall, heavy bleeding, or any life-threatening condition — go to the nearest emergency department immediately or call local emergency services.

Emergency guidance and appointment requests are separate: please do not use the appointment form for emergencies.

Our Injury Pathway

Injuries are assessed with examination and imaging as indicated. Whether a fracture is treated in a cast, with realignment, or with surgery depends on the injury and the individual — this is decided in discussion with you.

Follow-up and rehabilitation planning are part of the pathway, not an afterthought.

What We Treat

Common Injuries

Fractures

Assessment and management of broken bones, simple or complex.

Dislocations

Care for joints that have moved out of place.

Soft-tissue injuries

Ligament, tendon, and muscle injuries examined and treated.

Fall and accident injuries

Musculoskeletal injuries after falls, road incidents, or sport.

Follow-up care

Planned reviews to confirm healing and guide return to activity.

Please contact the hospital to confirm current emergency and injury-care availability before travelling.

Seek Assessment

If you have an injury that is not an emergency, book a consultation so it can be assessed properly.
